01 / Models

Three Approaches to
Stock Prediction

Comparing classical deep learning with quantum-classical hybrid and fully quantum architectures for time-series forecasting.

Classical

Classical GAN

LSTM-based Generator with MLP Discriminator. Uses sequential pattern recognition for time-series forecasting with adversarial training.

Generator LSTM (2-layer, 64 hidden)
Discriminator MLP (64→32→1)
Training 50 epochs, BCELoss
Hybrid Quantum

Hybrid QGAN

Quantum circuit generator with classical discriminator. RY encoding and parameterized Rot gates with entangling CNOT layers.

Generator Quantum Circuit (3 qubits)
Discriminator Classical MLP
Diff Method Parameter-Shift Rule
Fully Quantum

Fully Quantum GAN

End-to-end quantum architecture using SWAP test for discrimination. Amplitude encoding with quantum state overlap measurement.

Architecture CSWAP + Hadamard Test
Qubits 5 total (2 gen + 2 real + ancilla)
Encoding Amplitude Embedding

04 / Architecture

Technical Deep Dive

End-to-end pipeline from raw FTSE data to stock price predictions.

01

Data Ingestion

Load FTSE stock prices from CSV. Apply Hodrick-Prescott filter (λ=1600) to extract market trend, removing cyclical noise.

statsmodels.hpfilter → trend extraction
02

Normalization

MinMaxScaler scales to [0,1]. Per-sequence L2 normalization ensures quantum state compatibility (unit-norm vectors).

MinMaxScaler → L2 norm per window
03

Windowing

Create sliding windows: 3 past time steps → 1 future prediction. 80/20 train-test split for evaluation.

past=3, future=1, split=80%
04

GAN Training

Adversarial training with Generator producing predictions and Discriminator distinguishing real from generated sequences.

Adam (β₁=0.5, β₂=0.999)
05

Prediction & Evaluation

Inverse-transform predictions back to original price scale. Evaluate with RMSE, MAE, and R² metrics.

scaler.inverse_transform → metrics
